- (decision) AI Study Buddy is an IBE160 autumn-2026 group-project web prototype for students, intended to establish a realistic MVP before detailed planning and implementation.
- (decision) The proposed outcome is study resources from uploaded notes, slides, curriculum PDFs, or text: summaries, flashcards, quiz Q&A, key concepts, and source page pointers where possible.
- (assumption) LLM choice, confidence handling, summary granularity, table/figure treatment, and local-versus-cloud processing remain open; privacy and authentication are important if material is stored or shared.
- (decision) Team selected the Coaching path: work section by section, challenge unclear assumptions, and do not infer requirements; unresolved items remain explicitly undecided until confirmed.
- (decision) Problem framing confirmed: higher-education students spend time manually reading and structuring course material into study resources; lost study time and difficulty structuring/reviewing large material volumes are the primary consequences.
- (assumption) Primary use case, priority of poor understanding and recall, and a narrower initial student segment/course type remain undecided.
- (override) Official assignment is authoritative: scope is higher-education students processing PDF/text curriculum material into specified study resources; no primary study scenario is defined, and relative problem priorities remain undecided.
- (decision) Confirmed assignment constraints: inputs include PDF/text material plus course code/subject, detail level, and language/preferences; outputs are summaries, flashcards, quiz Q&A, key concepts, and source/page references; login/security only if material is stored/shared; no commerce.
- (assumption) LLM/confidence level, summary granularity, table/figure handling, and local-versus-cloud processing remain undecided technical decision points.
- (decision) MVP confirms generated study outputs are provided view-only; the assignment requires generation and provision but does not require editing, regeneration, export, or persistent storage.
- (assumption) Automatic versus user-selected generation, editing/regeneration, export, saved outputs, and one-versus-multiple-file processing remain undecided.
- (decision) MVP users are higher-education students using their own curriculum material; lecturers and administrators are explicitly out of scope.
- (assumption) Additional student roles, sharing between students, and a specific student success outcome after one use remain undecided.
- (decision) No specific product differentiator is assignment-defined. Source/page references, preferences, detail level, and focused MVP scope are capabilities/constraints, not unique differentiators.
- (assumption) Whether the team will define a product differentiator later remains undecided.
- (decision) Confirmed success criteria: a student can provide specified inputs and receive every assignment-specified output type; source/page references must be provided.
- (assumption) Specific quality, usability, reliability, processing-time, privacy, and security standards beyond conditional login/security remain undecided.
- (decision) MVP has no further explicit exclusions: commerce and lecturer/administrator roles are out of scope; editing, regeneration, export, saving/accounts, sharing, multi-file processing, and table/figure handling remain undecided and not excluded.
- (decision) Vision is explicitly undecided beyond the course-project MVP; no future direction, roadmap, or future features are confirmed.
- (event) Draft assembled from confirmed assignment scope and user decisions, with unresolved items separated into Open Decisions rather than treated as requirements.
- (event) Applied configured structure and prose polish: condensed duplicate inventories and clarified wording without changing requirements, scope, or open decisions.
